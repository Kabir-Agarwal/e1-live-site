How it works, in one picture

  1. DataMinute-by-minute prices for every stock
  2. Rulers41 measures: momentum, volatility, volume, trend…
  3. HuntSearch the oldest 70% of history for repeats
  4. TestRe-check survivors on the next 20%
  5. ExamOne look at the locked newest 10%
  6. SignalsA passed pattern fires on today's prices
  7. Three lightsBoard · money · moment
  8. Paper tradeSimulated entry, stop and target

1. It measures. For each stock, the system turns raw prices into 41 simple measures — how fast price is moving, how jumpy it is, how busy trading is — at eight speeds, from one minute to one week.

2. It hunts, but only in the past. It splits each stock's history in time order. It searches only the oldest 70% for situations that were followed by the same outcome more often than normal.

3. It tests without peeking. Anything it finds is re-checked on the next 20%, which the search never saw. Most ideas die here, and that is the point.

4. It sits one exam. The newest 10% is locked away. It is opened once, never used to adjust anything, and every opening is logged. What survives is a validated pattern.

5. It trades on paper, with three lights. When a validated pattern fires on today's prices, it only becomes a paper trade if the pattern passed its exam, its edge beats the fees, and a real price is there. Every trade has a stop and a target fixed at entry.

The honesty rules

  • Nothing on this site is typed in by hand — every number is read from the system's own files.
  • Anything not yet known says “loading”; when the market is shut, the live panels say so.
  • Fees are subtracted before anything is called profitable.
  • The locked exam data is opened once per round and never used to tune.
  • Failures are published, not hidden — including the system's own mistakes.

What this site does NOT claim

  • Paper only. No real money. No broker orders. There is no order-placing code in the system.
  • No live track record yet. The first live paper session is Tuesday 15 September 2026.
  • No capital. Nothing here is an offer, a recommendation, or investment advice.
  • Today's list only. Results are measured on stocks that still trade, which flatters them.
  • Linked stocks counted separately. When many related stocks fire together, the evidence is thinner than the count suggests.
