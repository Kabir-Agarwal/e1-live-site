How it works
The whole desk in six lines.
1. It watches 210 stocks
Every 2 minutes, 35 readings per stock: where the price sits, momentum, calm, money flow, volume, the price gap.
2. Patterns are tested, not believed
A pattern earns a place only after months it never saw, day-by-day forward tests, and beating what luck alone could show.
3. Three engines trade, on paper
E1 trades the patterns at ₹1,000. E10 resizes by 323 tested facts. E11 resizes by pattern family. Each has its own ₹2,00,000 account.
4. Safety first
No order until the stock's price gap is measured and the other side of the trade is really there. Failing audits stop everything. Real money is closed.
5. Everything is written down
Every call, its reason, its result. Every message. Every day of data. Nothing typed by hand - all from the records.
6. The scoreboard decides
Engines earn or lose their place on live paper results. Failed ideas keep a diary and can come back only by proving it twice.
