How it works

The idea

The desk is a set of separate traders, each with its own paper account and its own way of looking at the market. None of them uses real money. Every number on this site is measured on real history or on simulated fills, after the real costs of buying and selling.

How a behaviour becomes a pattern

The desk searches each stock's own history for behaviours that tend to repeat. A behaviour becomes a pattern only if it passes three checks: it works on the test years, it still works on months sealed away and never looked at while building, and the move it predicts is big enough to clear the cost of the trade. Behaviours that fail go to a watch-list, not the bin.

Costs are real

Every test charges the true cost of trading: the gap between the buy and sell price, measured from the real order book where the desk has recorded it; the price you push the market by trading size; and the exchange and tax charges on every trade.

Paper only

Nothing here is a recommendation and nothing is real money.
