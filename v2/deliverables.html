Dubai Strategies

Buys a stock the morning after it looks quietly bought up: heavy trading and heavy delivery (shares actually taken away, not day-traded), a delivery share above its own normal and rising, a calm narrow day, and a price that held or rose. Long only, on paper, with the real cost of buying and selling. It is sold when the signal is gone after a couple of sessions, or at a stop set to twice the stock's own daily swing.

A star would mark a match backed by a bulk/block deal or a holding change — that data is not on the desk yet, so nothing is starred.

Index tests

Each index idea — holding the whole market for days, or trading it in and out the same day — was decided on older years and then tested on newer months it never saw, after the real cost of buying and selling, so the result cannot be flattered by hindsight.
