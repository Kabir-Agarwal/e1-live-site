+

+  thing. A claim about a test result with no command is unproven.
+- **Cite `file:line`** for anything you checked by reading — the exact path and line number, not "the code" or
+  "somewhere in the module".
+- **A claim with neither a command nor a `file:line` is not a finding — it is an opinion, and it is a FAIL.**

+  what they checked has not checked it. Say `UNCHECKABLE — <reason>` (a store that is missing, gitignored, or
+  never tracked) rather than banking a doubt as a pass; fail closed.
+
