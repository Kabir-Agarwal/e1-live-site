## 4. Why each variant beat or lost to flat

- **NORMAL:** It sizes the higher-hit-rate families up. But hit-rate is not rupee-profitability — the book's biggest earners win through a few large winners, not a high hit-rate, so NORMAL starves them and feeds high-hit-rate-but-thin families. Net vs flat on the crossing fill: -11.65R.
- **INVERSE:** It sizes the higher-hit-rate families DOWN — the mirror of NORMAL. That partly protects the book from the high-hit-rate-but-unprofitable families, so it beats NORMAL, but it also shrinks genuinely good families and still does not beat leaving every fire at a flat 1R. Net vs flat on the crossing fill: -2.85R.
